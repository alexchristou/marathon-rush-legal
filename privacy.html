Privacy Policy

Marathon Rush · by Alex Christou · Last updated 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Marathon Rush game (the "Game") collects, why, and what you can do about it. The Game is made by Alex Christou ("I", "me"). If you have a question, write to tzapps1@gmail.com.

What the Game stores

  • Your game progress: runner name, chosen runner, medals, gear, gels, race times and splits, settings (sound, vibration), and your free-race / ad-race count. It is kept on your device and, when you are online, backed up to Unity Cloud Save so it can be restored.
  • A player ID: when you first open the Game, Unity Authentication creates an anonymous player ID for you. No name, email or phone number is needed for this.
  • Leaderboard entries: when you finish a race online, your runner name, best time, splits and shoe choice are stored on the leaderboard. Your runner name and best time are visible to other players in the world top 10. Choose a runner name that doesn't identify you if you prefer.
  • Device check: to keep the leaderboard fair, a finished race is sent with a proof from Google Play Integrity (Android) or Apple App Attest (iPhone) that it comes from the genuine Game on a genuine device. These checks are used only to decide whether a time may enter the leaderboard.

Signing in (optional)

Settings ▸ "Save progress · Sign in" lets you link a Unity Player Account so your progress follows you to a new phone. Unity Player Accounts is run by Unity Technologies; if you sign in, Unity processes your account details (such as your email address) under its own terms. The Game itself only receives your Unity player ID. You can delete a Unity Player Account at player-account.unity.com/delete-account; Unity's privacy contact is unity-player-login-privacy@unity3d.com.

Advertising

The Game shows ads from Google AdMob (a banner, and an optional rewarded video to unlock extra races). Google may use your device's advertising identifier and similar data to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, the UK and Switzerland) the Game asks for your consent first, using Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your phone's settings.

Who processes the data

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Leaderboards; Unity Player Accounts) — Unity's privacy policy.
  • Google (AdMob ads and consent; Play Integrity on Android) — Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services.
  • Apple (App Attest on iPhone) — Apple's privacy policy.

These providers may process data on servers outside your country. I don't sell your personal information, and I don't use it for anything other than running the Game, keeping the leaderboard fair and showing ads.

What the Game doesn't collect

No location, contacts, photos, microphone, health or fitness data, and no payment details (the Game has no in-app purchases).

How long it's kept

Progress and leaderboard entries are kept while your player exists. Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game.

Your choices and rights

  • Settings ▸ Reset progress erases your game progress; signing out starts a new runner on your phone.
  • To have your player ID, cloud progress and leaderboard entries deleted, or to ask what is stored about you, email tzapps1@gmail.com with your runner name (and, if you can, the approximate date of your best time). Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR) you may also have the right to correct your data, object to its use, and complain to your data-protection authority.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent where you live), and Unity Player Accounts must not be used by them without verified parental consent. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact me and it will be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
